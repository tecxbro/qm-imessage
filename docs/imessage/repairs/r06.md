## Projection behavior

Every selected Photon candidate and direct Photon fallback passes through `isPhotonDestination()` and is reconstructed by `createPhotonDestination()` from the frozen allowlist. The projection preserves `type`, `target`, `audienceScopeId`, `conversation`, `conversationKind`, `principalIds`, the matching DM recipient or group identity, `onBehalfOf`, and an optional same-conversation `providerMessage`.

The projector discards capability-menu fields and unknown execution metadata. Invalid provider, installation, line, conversation, principal, audience, recipient, group, message, or `onBehalfOf` data returns `{ ok: false }`. Malformed candidate collections and entries do not throw. A matching malformed Photon candidate fails at the Photon boundary and is never projected as Slack.

The non-Photon projection is unchanged: a selected candidate retains only `type`, `target`, and a truthy `audienceScopeId`, while a direct non-Photon destination remains byte-identical. Valid key and default-key selection semantics are unchanged.
