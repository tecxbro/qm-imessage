## Storage behavior

`createPhotonReceiptStore()` discovers only captured receipts and processing receipts whose persisted claim lease expired at or before the injected `now`. Queries require exact provider and installation scope. `lineId: undefined` maps only to the line-less sentinel and never broadens the query to all lines.

Discovery validates canonical timestamps and limits of 1 through 128, orders by `captured_at, event_id`, fetches one extra row, and emits a keyset continuation only when another page exists. Decoded records must agree with indexed provider, installation, line, event, sequence, state, capture time, claim fence, and claim expiry columns. Missing or inconsistent claim data fails closed.

## Intake behavior

Provider intake performs bounded durable discovery after obtaining the existing intake ownership and starting live or catch-up streams. A completed sweep restarts without a cursor, which makes an insert behind the prior cursor visible. Discovery does not claim a receipt, invoke QM execution directly, or advance a contiguous checkpoint. Recovered envelopes re-enter the existing serialized handoff. Terminal rows and live foreign claims are skipped after a fresh read. A reference payload fails with `PROVIDER_RECOVERY_REFERENCE_UNRESOLVED` because no approved payload resolver exists.

Advanced recovery co-sorts durable, catch-up, and concurrently captured live envelopes before handoff. Spectrum recovery deduplicates overlap between durable discovery and live delivery. Event and byte bounds stay finite, cancellation prevents later handoff, and intake failure closes the existing streams.
